Artist Application for Diaspora: NYC Hispanic Artists Networking Event and Show

Diaspora: NYC Hispanic Artist Network is a organisation wishing to connect artists across the spanish speaking diaspora, to encourage collaboration and expand opportunites for artists. Our Hispanic Artists Networking Event and Gallery Show is a 3 day event from June 1-3, 2025. There will be networking booths with industry professionals, portfolio critiques and a Gallery to show some of your artworks.
